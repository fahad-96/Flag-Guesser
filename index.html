    Settings

    How to play

    A flag appears with four country names. Pick the right one. That is the whole game, but the details decide your score.

    Modes

    • Classic. Three lives and twelve seconds per flag. Run out of either and the round ends.
    • Time Attack. Sixty seconds total. Wrong answers cost three seconds.
    • Practice. No pressure. Scores are not ranked.

    Scoring

    • 100 points per correct flag, plus up to 50 for answering quickly.
    • Each consecutive correct answer adds 10% to your multiplier, up to double points at a 10 streak.
    • Difficulty multiplies everything: Easy x1, Medium x1.25, Hard x1.5, Expert x2.
    • A capital hint halves the points for that flag. Using 50/50 keeps three quarters.

    Keyboard

    • 1 to 4 choose an answer
    • Enter next flag
    • H capital hint
    • F 50/50
    • Esc pause
